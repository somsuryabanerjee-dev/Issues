Conclusion: The "Brain Drain" Reality

The data presents a sobering reality: West Bengal has shifted from an industrial powerhouse to an exporter of human capital. With a debt burden of ₹6.9 Lakh Crore and a lack of high-tech jobs, the state's greatest asset—its youth—is forced to migrate. Whether as migrant labor in Kerala or tech professionals in Bengaluru, the "Brain Drain" is the defining legacy of the current economic stagnation.
